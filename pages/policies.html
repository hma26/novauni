Policies & Accessibility

How we handle your information, our commitment to a safe and equitable campus, and how we make this website usable for everyone.

Privacy Policy

NOVA University collects only the information you choose to share with us through the application, inquiry and newsletter forms on this website: your name, e-mail address, phone number and the details of your request.

  • We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry or process your application.
  • We never sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • You may request a copy of, or the deletion of, your data at any time by e-mailing privacy@nova.edu.

Title IX Compliance

NOVA University does not discriminate on the basis of sex in its education programs and activities, in line with Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972. Reports of sexual harassment or discrimination may be made to the Title IX Coordinator at titleix@nova.edu or by calling +1 (800) 555-7233 (24/7 Campus Security Dispatch).

Accessibility Statement

We want every visitor to be able to use this website. It has been built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA:

  • All pages can be navigated with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to main content” link.
  • Every image has a text alternative and the campus tour video has a text description.
  • Text and background colours meet the minimum contrast ratio, and the layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.

If you experience any difficulty using this site, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
