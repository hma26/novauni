About NOVA University

A Legacy of Excellence, Guided by Tomorrow’s Innovations.

Founded in 1974, NOVA University stands at the intersection of rigorous academic inquiry and real-world societal impact. Our world-renowned faculty members mentor over 14,000 undergraduate and graduate scholars across 6 multi-disciplinary faculties.

  • Hands-On Research Day 1: Access 20+ specialized laboratories and $85M+ in annual research funding.
  • Global Industry Connections: Direct internships with Fortune 500 partners and Silicon Valley innovation hubs.
  • Inclusive Campus Community: Over 120 student organizations, Greek life, NCAA athletics, and international cultural clubs.
